# Weekly roundup rules (personal)

Your own rules. Team rules live in the team repo (`team_rules_file`); where the two conflict, these win.

**Format:** `- **ID** [personal] rule`. IDs are permanent and never reused. The learning step proposes new rules from your corrections. Each one names the rule it replaces or merges with, or says `new`, and nothing is written without your approval.
